Shaha Dolimov (full name Shahbozbek / Shohbozbek Dolimov) was born on February 16, 1994, in Tashkent, Uzbekistan. He is a marketer, entrepreneur, blogger and author.

Education: Bachelor of Business Administration in Business Administration and Management at Ritsumeikan Asia Pacific University in Japan (2012–2016), then a Master of Science in Strategic Marketing Management at BI Norwegian Business School in Oslo, Norway (2016–2018).

Meraki: In March 2020 he founded Meraki Marketing Agency in Tashkent, a digital marketing agency for real estate developers, and serves as its Founder & CEO. Under his leadership Meraki grew from a 3-person team with 1 client to a team of more than 20 people with 10+ clients. Meraki's team has included Aziz Normukhamedov (Sales Manager) and Bekhruz Shamuratov (Head of Marketing). He also runs Meraki Academy, an online course platform.

Dolimov: LinkedIn Ads Agency. He later founded Dolimov, an agency focused on LinkedIn Ads for B2B companies. He has also worked as a Sales and Marketing Executive at QiSpace (from September 2022).

Book: He is the author of "Maqsad" ("Goal"), a book in Uzbek about the difficulties he faced while studying in Japan and Norway and how he overcame them. He wrote it to prepare young people before they go abroad and to increase their self-confidence. It is also available as an audiobook.

Public work: He teaches and speaks about marketing, including a Najot Ta'lim master class on "Marketing agentlik ochish va tizimlashtirish" (opening and systematizing a marketing agency), a free intensive on effective marketing strategy and current marketing trends, and a talk on mistakes in communication marketing for Marketing Ligasi. He has been an ambassador for Millat Umidi University. He runs a YouTube channel (@shahadolimov) with vlogs and personal podcasts on personal growth, productivity and marketing, and has appeared on podcasts such as Azam Podcast and NmaGap. He writes on his Telegram channel (@shahadolimov), mostly in Uzbek; an older channel of his is @shahadolimov_oilasi. He also posts on LinkedIn and Instagram (@shahadolimov).
